# Important CPC Meetings and the Evolution of the Socialist Economic System

## Must-Know Before Reading: Naming Conventions of the Party's Important Meetings

In socialist economics you will frequently encounter expressions such as "the Third Plenary Session of the Eleventh Central Committee," "the Fourteenth Congress," and "the Third Plenary Session of the Eighteenth Central Committee." These names look complex, but in fact they follow a clear set of naming conventions.

### What does "the Xth Congress" mean?

**"The Xth Congress"** = the Xth **National Congress** of the Communist Party of China.

- The National Congress is the Party's highest leadership body, convened once every five years.
- For example: **"the Twentieth Congress"** = the Twentieth National Congress of the Communist Party of China (held in 2022).
- The First National Congress of the Communist Party of China (**the First Congress**) was held in Shanghai in 1921, marking the Party's formal founding.

### What does "the Xth Central Committee" mean?

**"The Xth Central Committee"** = the **term number of the Central Committee** elected by a given National Congress.

- One National Congress elects one term of the Central Committee.
- For example: **the Twentieth Central Committee** was elected by the Twentieth National Congress.
- Each term of the Central Committee also lasts five years (matching the interval between two National Congresses).

### What does "the Xth Plenary Session" mean?

**"The Xth Plenary Session"** = the abbreviated form of the Yth **Plenary Session** of the Xth Central Committee.

- Each Central Committee, during its five-year term, usually holds seven plenary sessions (from the First to the Seventh Plenary Session), convened one or two times per year.
- For example: **the Third Plenary Session of the Eleventh Central Committee** = the **Third** Plenary Session of the Eleventh Central Committee of the Communist Party of China.

### Conventional Functions of Each Plenary Session

Since reform and opening up, the agendas of successive plenary sessions have formed fairly fixed conventions:

| Plenary Session | Time Held (Approximate) | Main Function |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| **First Plenary Session** | Immediately after the close of the National Congress | Elects the Central Political Bureau, the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau, and the General Secretary of the Central Committee |
| **Second Plenary Session** | Before the following year's "Two Sessions" | Recommends candidates for leadership posts of state institutions; discusses reform of the State Council's institutional structure |
| **Third Plenary Session** | Second half of the second year of the term | Focuses on **economic reform and major institutional change** (the greatest impact on economic policy) |
| **Fourth Plenary Session** | Third year of the term | Focuses on major strategic issues such as Party building, the rule of law, and the national governance system |
| **Fifth Plenary Session** | Fourth year of the term | Deliberates on recommendations for the five-year plan |
| **Sixth Plenary Session** | First half of the fifth year of the term | Focuses on Party building and major historical resolutions |
| **Seventh Plenary Session** | Before the next National Congress | Prepares for the next National Congress |

### What This Means for Reading This Material

Once you understand this naming system, you will see that:

1. **"The Xth Congress"** (such as the Fourteenth or Fifteenth Congress) decides **directional questions** — setting reform goals, guiding ideas, and development strategy.
2. **"The Yth Plenary Session of the Xth Central Committee"** (such as the Third Plenary Session of the Eleventh Central Committee, or the Third Plenary Session of the Fourteenth Central Committee) works between two National Congresses to make **concrete arrangements and institutional design** for the direction set by the Congress.
3. Of these, the **Third Plenary Session** is most closely tied to economic reform — the Third Plenary Session of the Eleventh Central Committee in 1978 opened reform and opening up, the Third Plenary Session of the Fourteenth Central Committee in 1993 built the framework of the market economic system, and the Third Plenary Session of the Eighteenth Central Committee in 2013 deployed comprehensive deep reform.

## Timeline of Core Meetings

### March 1949

Meeting name: Second Plenary Session of the Seventh Central Committee of the CPC

What was done:

- Proposed that the **Party's center of work shift from the countryside to the cities**, with city work taking production and construction as its center.
- Set the basic policies of the new China in politics, economy, and foreign affairs, and pointed out the direction of China's transformation from an agrarian country into an industrial country and from a New-Democratic society into a socialist society.
- Proposed concrete economic policies such as confiscating bureaucratic capital and utilizing and restricting private capital.

What was advanced:

- This was a key meeting on the eve of the founding of the People's Republic of China, making policy preparation for the establishment of the socialist economic system.
- It clarified the basic path of transition from a New-Democratic economy to a socialist economy, and is the starting point for the subsequent construction of the socialist economic system.

### September 1956

Meeting name: Eighth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Made a scientific judgment of the principal domestic contradiction after the socialist transformation was basically complete: the **contradiction between the people's demand for an advanced industrial country and the reality of a backward agrarian country**, and the **contradiction between the people's demand for rapid economic and cultural development and the present state in which economic and cultural life cannot satisfy the people's needs**.
- Proposed the economic construction guideline of **"opposing both conservatism and adventurism, advancing steadily amid overall balance"**.
- Adopted the recommendations on developing the second five-year plan for the national economy.
- Chen Yun put forward his famous **"Three Mainstays and Three Supplements"** economic thought at the congress:
  - State-run and collectively-run operations are the mainstay, supplemented by a certain amount of individual operations;
  - Planned production is the mainstay, supplemented by free production within the range permitted by the state, in accordance with changes in the market;
  - The state market is the mainstay, supplemented by a state-guided free market within a certain range.

What was advanced:

- This is an extremely important milestone in socialist economics. The Eighth Congress made, for the first time, a scientific judgment of the principal social contradiction during the socialist construction period, clarifying that class struggle was no longer the principal contradiction.
- Chen Yun's "Three Mainstays and Three Supplements" was an important breakthrough against the Soviet model of a highly centralized planned economy, reflecting an early attempt to explore market regulation within the framework of a planned economy, and provided an important precursor to later theoretical innovation during reform and opening up.
- But the line of the Eighth Congress could not be well implemented afterward (the Anti-Rightist Campaign, the Great Leap Forward, etc.), and it was not until the Third Plenary Session of the Eleventh Central Committee that the focus returned to economic construction.

### December 1978

Meeting name: Third Plenary Session of the Eleventh Central Committee of the CPC

What was done:

- Shifted the center of the work of the Party and the state to socialist modernization.
- Formally opened reform and opening up, beginning to break through the highly centralized planned economic system.
- Opened up the practical space for the subsequent exploration of economic reform centered on "what socialism is and how to build socialism."

What was advanced:

- It did not directly propose "the socialist market economy," but it completed a directional turn.
- The subsequent discussions on the primary stage of socialism, the commodity economy, market mechanisms, and the granting of power and concessions to enterprises all unfolded after this turn.

### September 1982

Meeting name: Twelfth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Formally proposed "building socialism with Chinese characteristics."
- In the guiding ideology of economic system reform, formally formed the formulation of "planning as the mainstay, market regulation as the auxiliary."
- Advanced reform from general granting of power and concessions to the question of how to handle the relationship between planning and the market.

What was advanced:

- Compared with the earlier simple reflection on the old system, this meeting began to answer head-on whether socialism can make use of the market.
- But at this point the market was still placed in an auxiliary position, and theoretically the framework of "planning above the market" had not yet been broken through.

### October 1984

Meeting name: Third Plenary Session of the Twelfth Central Committee of the CPC

What was done:

- Adopted the *Decision of the Central Committee of the CPC on Reform of the Economic System*.
- Clarified that the socialist economy is "a planned commodity economy on the foundation of public ownership."
- Pushed the focus of reform further from the countryside into the cities, driving systematic adjustments in enterprise reform, price reform, and reform of the distribution system.

What was advanced:

- This was an important advance over the view that "the market is only a supplement," beginning to recognize that the commodity economy is not the patent of capitalism.
- Theory moved forward a step, but it still emphasized "planned," indicating that the status of market mechanisms had not yet been raised to a general method of resource allocation.

### October 1987

Meeting name: Thirteenth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Systematically expounded the theory of the primary stage of socialism.
- Proposed that, under the socialist commodity economy, planning and the market should be inherently unified and mutually combined.
- Proposed the line of economic operation that "the state regulates the market, and the market guides enterprises."

What was advanced:

- It went further than the Twelfth Congress and the Third Plenary Session of the Twelfth Central Committee, no longer merely acknowledging the commodity economy but beginning to discuss the mechanistic role of the market in the whole economic operation.
- The theoretical focus shifted from "whether to allow the market to exist" to "how the state, the market, and enterprises form a new operating structure."

### January to February 1992

Meeting name: Deng Xiaoping's Southern Tour Talks

What was done:

- Addressing the "capitalist or socialist" dispute in reform, clearly pointed out that planning and the market are both economic means and not the essential markers distinguishing socialism from capitalism.
- Put forward the key standard for judging the gains and losses of reform: whether it is conducive to developing the productive forces, strengthening comprehensive national strength, and raising the people's standard of living.
- Provided a key intellectual breakthrough for establishing the goal of the socialist market economy.

What was advanced:

- This was the key breakthrough in theory.
- It directly broke the old framework of "a bit more market means capitalism," enabling the Party to reunderstand planning and the market from the standpoint of instrumental rationality.

### October 1992

Meeting name: Fourteenth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Formally proposed that the goal of China's economic system reform is to establish a socialist market economic system.
- Raised market mechanisms from an "auxiliary tool" to a constituent principle of the socialist economic system.
- Completed a major shift in reform goals from "planned commodity economy" to "socialist market economy."

What was advanced:

- This is a landmark turning point in socialist economic theory.
- Compared with earlier formulations, this meeting was the first time the Party's formal documents explicitly combined the market economy with the socialist system.

### November 1993

Meeting name: Third Plenary Session of the Fourteenth Central Committee of the CPC

What was done:

- Adopted the *Decision of the Central Committee of the CPC on Several Issues Concerning the Establishment of a Socialist Market Economic System*.
- Made systematic arrangements for the basic framework of the socialist market economic system.
- Set out reform tasks around the modern enterprise system, a unified and open market system, a macroeconomic regulation system, the income distribution system, and the social security system.

What was advanced:

- The Fourteenth Congress resolved the question of goals; this meeting resolved the question of the institutional framework.
- Theory began to convert into institutional design; the socialist market economy was no longer only a statement of principles, but entered the stage of operable reform plans.

### September 1997

Meeting name: Fifteenth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Further emphasized that building a socialist economy with Chinese characteristics means developing a market economy under socialist conditions.
- Established Deng Xiaoping Theory as the Party's guiding ideology.
- Advanced SOE reform, the adjustment of the ownership structure, and the further maturation of the theory of the basic economic system.

What was advanced:

- What the Fifteenth Congress emphasized was not "whether to have a market," but "how to develop a market economy under socialist conditions."
- The theoretical focus began to shift from establishing the institutional goal to deepening the basic economic system, the ownership structure, and the modern enterprise system.

### November 2002

Meeting name: Sixteenth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Reaffirmed adherence to the reform direction of the socialist market economy.
- Clarified that the market should play a foundational role in resource allocation under state macroeconomic regulation.
- Advanced the perfection of the socialist market economic system and linked reform with the goal of fully building a moderately prosperous society.

What was advanced:

- The Sixteenth Congress continued to stabilize expectations along the established reform direction, emphasizing both market mechanisms and macroeconomic regulation.
- It resolved the question of "how the state continues to play a role after the market's role expands."

### November 2013

Meeting name: Third Plenary Session of the Eighteenth Central Committee of the CPC

What was done:

- Adopted the *Decision of the Central Committee of the CPC on Several Major Issues Concerning Comprehensive Deep Reform*.
- Proposed that the market play a decisive role in resource allocation, and that the government's role be better fulfilled.
- Advanced reform from single-track economic system reform into comprehensive deep reform across economics, politics, culture, society, ecological civilization, and Party building.

What was advanced:

- This was a further development of the Sixteenth Congress's formulation of a "foundational role."
- Theoretically, the market's resource-allocation function was further elevated; at the same time, it was made clear that the government does not withdraw, but shifts from direct allocation to rule-making, public services, macroeconomic regulation, and market supervision.

### October 2017

Meeting name: Nineteenth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Proposed that China's economy has shifted from a stage of high-speed growth to a stage of high-quality development.
- Proposed building a modernized economic system.
- Emphasized perfecting the socialist market economic system and advancing supply-side structural reform.

- This means the problem awareness of socialist economics has changed.
- The focus shifted further from "whether there is a market and how much role it plays" to "how, in the new development stage, to improve the quality of development, optimize structure, and handle the relations among efficiency and fairness, and growth and security."

### October 2022

Meeting name: Twentieth National Congress of the CPC

What was done:

- Emphasized that high-quality development is the top priority for comprehensively building a modernized socialist country.
- Emphasized building a high-level socialist market economic system.

- The Twentieth Congress placed the question of the socialist market economic system within the overall framework of Chinese-style modernization and high-quality development.
