# Books

The history of economics is written not only in papers but in books: *The Wealth of Nations* drew the boundary of the discipline, *The General Theory* reversed the direction of policy in the middle of the Great Depression, and *Capital in the Twenty-First Century* put inequality back at the centre of the agenda. Here are thirteen representative works laid out by year of publication — from 1776 to 2013, each book marking one turn in the way the question was posed.
