# About this site

Hello Economics is the working record I left behind while sorting through economics material. The spine is what each theory had to answer when it was written, how it developed afterwards, and how data confirmed or corrected it. I keep the entry points I use most for review and looking things up here as well.

## Scope

- [Math foundations](/math/Readme) collects the mathematical tools economics actually uses: calculus for finding optima, linear algebra for solving equilibria, probability and statistics for testing, and optimization methods that connect the tools to economic questions. I keep it early in the navigation because it is convenient when looking up formulas.
- [Western economics](/western/Readme) is the main line: the [micro half](/western/需求与供给) runs from supply and demand to market failure, and the [macro half](/western/宏观经济活动与宏观经济学) runs from national income accounting to the disagreements between schools.
- Four specialist subjects, each readable on its own: [Money and banking](/monetary/Readme), [Public finance](/finance/Readme), [International economics](/international/Readme) and [Socialist economic theory](/socialist/Readme).
- [Books](/books) is a shelf of thirteen classics, each with a short review. The [glossary](/glossary) collects and defines terms and is searchable. [Economists](/people) maps the lineage network. The [theory timeline](/timeline) is draggable and zoomable, threading theories in the order they appeared.

The chapter sequences follow the standard Chinese textbook outlines; the prose is rewritten on that skeleton as exposition of the knowledge, and exam materials such as question banks and memorization mnemonics have been dropped.

## Notes on content and data

- Structure: courses and chapters follow the standard textbook outlines. My prose usually starts from the problem, tries to complete the chain of reasoning, and records the limits and the tests of each theory.
- Dates, facts and attributions are cross-checked against the site's economist entries, the timeline, and standard histories of economic thought; omissions are still possible — corrections are welcome.
- Formulas are typeset in LaTeX and validated for legal commands and delimiters at build time. Diagrams are hand-drawn line SVGs; figures credited to sources come from the textbooks they are cited from.
- Statistical readings with no single authoritative basis are marked as approximate or as "subject to the latest release".
- Claims in the docs are reconciled against the source code in batches; the three classes of discrepancy found and fixed are recorded in the [documentation consistency audit](./审计-文档一致性).
- The textbooks and bibliographies behind each course are listed in the [bibliography](/reference/参考书目); in-text quotations link back to the [reference](/reference/参考书目) material so sources stay checkable.
