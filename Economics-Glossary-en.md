## Western Economics

- 125 entries across the Western economics and international economics sections.

- **IS curve**: The IS curve is the curve describing the combinations of national income and the interest rate at which the product market is in equilibrium. [source](western/产品市场和货币市场的一般均衡.md)
- **LM curve**: The LM curve depicts the combinations of the interest rate and national income at which the money market is in equilibrium. [source](western/产品市场和货币市场的一般均衡.md)
- **Disposable personal income**: Disposable personal income (DPI) is the portion of personal income that is actually available for consumption and saving after personal income taxes are deducted. Formula: DPI = personal income − personal income taxes. [source](western/宏观经济活动与宏观经济学.md)
- **Condition for simultaneous Pareto optimality in exchange and production**: The condition for simultaneous Pareto optimality in exchange and production is that, for all goods, the marginal rate of substitution between any two goods equals the marginal rate of transformation in their production, namely $RCS_{XY}=RPT_{XY}$. [source](western/一般均衡论和福利经济学.md)
- **Condition for Pareto optimality in exchange**: The condition for Pareto optimality in exchange is that in exchange, the marginal rate of substitution between any pair of goods is the same for every individual who uses those two goods, namely $RCS_{12}^{A} = RCS_{12}^B$; at this point the society has reached the Pareto-optimal state of product distribution and thus achieves efficiency in exchange. [source](western/一般均衡论和福利经济学.md)
- **Rate of product transformation**: The marginal rate of transformation is the proportion by which the output of one good must be reduced in order to increase the output of another good by one unit. [source](western/一般均衡论和福利经济学.md)
- **Price discrimination**: Price discrimination refers to a monopolist selling the same good at different prices within the same period. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Price adjustment equation**: The price adjustment equation is the equation expressing the relationship between the inflation rate and the forces that generate inflationary pressure. [source](western/通货膨胀理论.md)
- **Supply**: Supply is the quantity of a good that producers are willing and able to offer at each possible price within a given period, other things being equal. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Price elasticity of supply**: The price elasticity of supply is, within a given period, the responsiveness of the quantity supplied of a good to a change in its price. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Law of supply**: The law of supply, also called the supply theorem, supply law, or supply principle, states that the quantity supplied by producers changes in the same direction as the price of the good. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Free-rider problem**: The free-rider problem is the market failure that arises in an economy because consumption satisfaction can be obtained without payment. [source](western/市场失灵和微观经济政策.md)
- **Public goods**: Goods that lack exclusivity or (and) rivalry, so that once produced some people cannot be excluded from consuming them, are called (pure) public goods. [source](western/市场失灵和微观经济政策.md)
- **Single monetary rule**: The single monetary rule is a monetarist policy proposition and the most important theoretical rule of monetarist economics. [source](western/宏观经济学的意见分歧.md)
- **Profit-maximization principle of the firm**: The profit-maximization principle is the general rule a firm follows when making production decisions: the additional revenue from each extra unit of output (or factor) must equal the additional cost it brings, that is, marginal revenue equals marginal cost: $MR =MC$. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Cournot model**: The Cournot model is a simple model containing only two oligopolistic firms, that is, a duopoly model. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Nominal and real national income**: (1) Nominal national income is national income computed at the current-year prices of goods and services; it does not take inflation into account. (2) Real national income is national income computed at the prices of some earlier base year, or national income deflated by a price index. [source](western/宏观经济活动与宏观经济学.md)
- **Backward-bending labor supply curve**: According to workers' optimizing behavior, for a given wage rate the worker determines the optimal labor supply at the point of utility maximization, thereby yielding the supply curve of labor. [source](western/生产要素市场.md)
- **Harrod-Domar model**: The Harrod-Domar model is a model for analyzing economic growth proposed successively by Harrod and Domar in the 1940s. [source](western/经济增长.md)
- **Marginal rate of substitution of commodities**: The marginal rate of substitution of commodities is the amount of the other good a consumer must give up in order to increase consumption of the first good by one unit while the utility level remains unchanged. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Gross domestic product**: Gross domestic product (GDP) is the total market value of all final goods and services produced by an economy (a country or a region) using its factors of production within a given period. [source](western/宏观经济活动与宏观经济学.md)
- **Gross national product**: Gross national product (GNP) is the total market value of final products produced by all the factors of production owned by a country's residents within a given period, that is, the total market value of final products produced by the country's permanent residents. [source](western/宏观经济活动与宏观经济学.md)
- **Equilibrium price**: The equilibrium price is the price at which the market demand for a good equals its market supply. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Monopolistically competitive market**: A monopolistically competitive market is a market in which many firms produce and sell differentiated products of the same kind; the market contains both monopoly and competition, being neither perfectly competitive nor perfectly monopolistic. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Externality**: An externality, also called an external economic effect, is the effect of one economic agent's activity on the welfare of another agent that is not reflected through market transactions. [source](western/市场失灵和微观经济政策.md)
- **External economies and external diseconomies**: External economies refer to a case in which one agent's economic activity brings benefits to other members of society while the agent receives no corresponding compensation, that is, the activity exerts positive external effects; external diseconomies refer to a case in which one agent's economic activity imposes costs on other members of society while the agent does not bear all of them, that is, the activity exerts negative external effects. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Unemployment rate**: The unemployment rate is the ratio of the number of unemployed to the total labor force (the employed plus the unemployed) within a given period. [source](western/宏观经济活动与宏观经济学.md)
- **Perfectly competitive market**: A perfectly competitive market is a market structure in which competition is unimpeded and undisturbed. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Oligopolistic market**: An oligopolistic market is a form of market organization in which only a few sellers exist in a given industry. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Partial equilibrium and general equilibrium**: Partial equilibrium is the equilibrium of the market for one particular product or factor, on the assumption that other markets are unchanged; general equilibrium is the state in which the supply and demand of all markets in an economic system reach equilibrium simultaneously. General equilibrium analysis proceeds from the behavior of microeconomic agents and examines the conditions required for the supply and demand of every product and every factor to reach equilibrium simultaneously, together with the corresponding equilibrium prices and equilibrium quantities. [source](western/一般均衡论和福利经济学.md)
- **Market clearing**: Market clearing means that both wages in the labor market and commodity prices in the product market are fully flexible and adjust rapidly according to supply and demand, so as to reach an equilibrium in which the two sides are equal. [source](western/宏观经济学的意见分歧.md)
- **Market failure**: Market failure means that the market mechanism fails to allocate resources efficiently. [source](western/市场失灵和微观经济政策.md)
- **Pareto-optimal state**: The Pareto-optimal state, also called economic efficiency, is the state in which no one can improve his or her own condition without making someone else worse off. [source](western/一般均衡论和福利经济学.md)
- **Average cost**: Average cost is the cost a firm incurs on average for each unit of output. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Average factor cost**: Average factor cost is the cost a firm pays on average for each unit of a factor of production it purchases. [source](western/生产要素市场.md)
- **Derived demand**: Derived demand refers to the demand by firms for factors of production that arises from consumers' demand for products. [source](western/生产要素市场.md)
- **Aggregate supply curve**: The aggregate supply curve is the curve showing the relationship between the economy's aggregate supply and the general price level; aggregate supply usually moves in the same direction as the general price level, so the curve slopes upward to the right. [source](western/总需求和总供给分析.md)
- **Total revenue, average revenue, and marginal revenue**: (1) A firm's total revenue ($TR$) is the entire income obtained by selling a given quantity of product at a given price, that is, $TR=p(y)\cdot y$. In the formula, $TR$ is total revenue, $p(y)$ is the price the firm faces when selling the product, and $y$ is total sales. (2) Average revenue ($AR$) is the revenue the firm obtains per unit of product sold on average, that is, $AR=TR/y=p(y)$; average revenue equals the price of the product, and the demand curve facing the firm is its average revenue curve. (3) Marginal revenue ($MR$) is the increment of revenue obtained when the firm sells one additional unit of product. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Aggregate demand curve**: The aggregate demand curve is the curve showing the correspondence between the economy's aggregate demand and the general price level; aggregate demand usually moves in the opposite direction to the general price level, so the curve slopes downward to the right. [source](western/总需求和总供给分析.md)
- **Cost-push inflation**: Cost-push inflation, also called cost inflation or supply-side inflation, is a sustained and significant rise in the general price level caused by increases in supply costs. [source](western/通货膨胀理论.md)
- **Investment function**: On the condition that the marginal efficiency of capital is unchanged, investment depends on the interest rate and is a decreasing function of it; investment and the interest rate move in opposite directions, expressed by the formula $I=I(r)$. [source](western/产品市场和货币市场的一般均衡.md)
- **Support price**: The support price, also called the floor price, is the minimum price the government sets for an industry's products in order to foster that industry's production. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Income indexation**: Income indexation is a measure the government adopts in dealing with cost-push inflation. [source](western/通货膨胀理论.md)
- **Income effect**: The income effect is the influence on the quantity demanded that arises because a change in a good's price changes the consumer's real income level and thereby causes the consumer to change the quantities consumed. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Utility**: Utility is the capacity of a good or service to satisfy human wants, that is, the degree of satisfaction a consumer feels when consuming the good or service. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Neoclassical growth model**: The neoclassical growth model is a model of national economic growth proposed by the American economist Solow and others. [source](western/经济增长.md)
- **New classical macroeconomics**: New classical macroeconomics, also called the "new classical" school, is a school of economics that follows the tradition of classical economics and believes in the effectiveness of market forces; it holds that if the market mechanism is allowed to function spontaneously, it can solve a series of macroeconomic problems such as unemployment and recession. [source](western/宏观经济学的意见分歧.md)
- **Indifference curve**: The indifference curve is an analytical device of ordinal utility theory used to represent all the combinations of quantities of two goods between which the consumer's preferences are identical. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Substitution effect**: The substitution effect is the change in the quantity demanded of a good that arises because the change in its price alters its relative price, and the change in relative price in turn alters the quantity demanded. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Warranted rate of growth**: The warranted rate of growth $G_w$ is given by the formula $G_w=\frac{S_d}{v_r}$, where $S_d$ is the desired saving rate (assumed given) and $v_r$ is the capital-output ratio entrepreneurs require as they envisage it. [source](western/经济增长.md)
- **Comparative static analysis**: Comparative static analysis is the method of comparing and analyzing different static equilibrium states. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Liquidity preference**: Liquidity preference, also called the preference for liquidity, is people's preference for holding money. [source](western/产品市场和货币市场的一般均衡.md)
- **Liquidity preference trap**: The liquidity trap, also called the Keynesian trap, is an economic state in which, when the interest rate is extremely low, people's speculative demand for money tends toward infinity, so that even if the monetary authority increases the money supply it can hardly push the interest rate down further and stimulate investment. [source](western/产品市场和货币市场的一般均衡.md)
- **Consumer price index**: The consumer price index is short for the index of consumer prices; it reflects changes in the price level of consumer goods (including services) and is generally compiled by the weighted average method. [source](western/通货膨胀理论.md)
- **Consumer equilibrium**: Consumer equilibrium is the state in which, other conditions unchanged, the consumer achieves maximum utility and will keep it unchanged. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Rational expectations hypothesis**: The rational expectations hypothesis holds that economic agents can form rational estimates of the future movements of economic variables such as prices, interest rates, profits, or income. [source](western/宏观经济学的意见分歧.md)
- **Walras's law**: Walras's law, also called Walras's rule, is an identity proposed by the economist Walras within his general equilibrium theory of perfectly competitive markets. [source](western/一般均衡论和福利经济学.md)
- **Condition for Pareto optimality in production**: The condition for Pareto optimality in production states that, for an economy with many individuals, many goods, and many factors, equilibrium requires on the production side that the marginal rate of technical substitution between any pair of factors be equal in the production of all goods produced with those two inputs, namely $RTS_{LK}^{1} = RTS_{LK}^2$; at this point the society has reached the Pareto-optimal state. [source](western/一般均衡论和福利经济学.md)
- **Optimal combination of factors of production**: The optimal combination of factors refers to the combination of factor quantities a producer uses, with production technology and factor prices unchanged, to maximize output at a given cost or to minimize cost at a given output. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Coase theorem**: The Coase theorem is a theory of property rights. [source](western/市场失灵和微观经济政策.md)
- **Isoquant curve**: The isoquant curve is the locus of the various combinations of the inputs of two factors of production that produce the same output under a constant level of technology. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Isocost equation**: A firm's isocost equation is the algebraic expression representing all the different factor combinations the firm can use at equal cost when factor prices are given. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Nash equilibrium**: A Nash equilibrium refers to a strategy combination in which no participant changes his or her own strategy as long as the other participants do not change theirs. [source](western/市场理论.md)
- **Natural rate hypothesis**: The natural rate hypothesis refers to the employment rate determined when the labor market reaches equilibrium under competitive conditions in the absence of interference from monetary factors. [source](western/宏观经济学的意见分歧.md)
- **Phillips curve**: The Phillips curve was first proposed by the British economist A.W. Phillips; his original study described the negative correlation between the unemployment rate and the growth rate of money wages, and only later was it often extended to the short-run trade-off between the inflation rate and the unemployment rate. [source](western/宏观经济学的意见分歧.md)
- **Increasing, constant, and decreasing returns to scale**: As a special case of economies and diseconomies of scale, if output is expanded by expanding the factors of production in the same proportion, the corresponding concept of returns to scale can be defined: (1) if output increases in a greater proportion than the factors of production, production exhibits increasing returns to scale; (2) if output increases in the same proportion as the factors, production exhibits constant returns to scale; (3) if output increases in a smaller proportion than the factors, production exhibits decreasing returns to scale. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Economies and diseconomies of scale**: Economies and diseconomies of scale describe the relationship between changes in a firm's output, and hence in its scale, and its costs. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Monetarism**: Monetarism, also called the monetarist school, takes curbing inflation and opposing state intervention as its main tenets and the modern quantity theory of money as its theoretical foundation; it firmly believes that changes in the money supply are the most fundamental cause of movements in the price level and economic activity, emphasizes the importance of money and monetary policy, and advocates a single-rule monetary policy. [source](western/宏观经济学的意见分歧.md)
- **Money wage rigidity**: Money wage rigidity is the phenomenon that money wages do not adjust rapidly in response to changes in labor demand and supply. [source](western/总需求和总供给分析.md)
- **Marginal efficiency of capital**: The marginal efficiency of capital is that discount rate which exactly equates the sum of the present values of the expected returns over the life of a capital asset with the supply price or replacement cost of that capital asset. [source](western/产品市场和货币市场的一般均衡.md)
- **Value of marginal product**: The value of marginal product is the value of the extra output obtained by adding one unit of a factor of production; it equals the marginal product multiplied by the price of the product, that is, $VMP=P \cdot MP$. [source](western/生产要素市场.md)
- **Marginal product**: The marginal product is the increment in total output obtained from each additional unit of a variable input when the production technology and other inputs are unchanged. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Marginal cost**: Marginal cost is the amount by which cost changes when output changes by a given quantity, that is, the increase in total cost when the firm adds one unit of output in the short run. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Marginal rate of technical substitution**: The marginal rate of technical substitution is the amount by which the input of one factor is reduced when the input of another factor is increased by one unit while the output level is maintained; its English abbreviation is $MRTS$. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Law of diminishing marginal rate of technical substitution**: The law of the diminishing marginal rate of technical substitution states that, with output held constant, as the input of one factor of production is continuously increased, the amount of the other factor that each additional unit of this factor can replace diminishes. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Marginal revenue product**: The marginal revenue product is the additional revenue obtained by adding one unit of a factor input when the inputs of all other factors of production are held fixed. [source](western/生产要素市场.md)
- **Law of diminishing marginal returns**: Under a constant level of technology, in the process of adding successive equal amounts of one variable factor of production to one or more factors whose quantities are fixed, when the input of the variable factor is below a certain specific value, the marginal product brought by additional input of the factor increases; when the input of the variable factor continues to increase beyond that specific value, the marginal product brought by additional input of the factor diminishes. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Law of diminishing marginal utility**: The law of diminishing marginal utility states that, within a given period and with the consumption of other goods unchanged, as a consumer keeps increasing the consumption of a certain good, the additional utility obtained from each additional unit of that good gradually decreases. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Marginal factor cost**: The marginal factor cost is the increase in cost that results when a firm adds one unit of a factor of production. [source](western/生产要素市场.md)
- **Adverse selection**: Adverse selection refers to the phenomenon in the lemons market whereby high-quality products are eliminated and low-quality products survive. [source](western/市场失灵和微观经济政策.md)
- **Moral hazard**: Moral hazard refers to behavior after a transaction contract is signed in which the better-informed party, in order to maximize its own gain, harms the other party while bearing none of the consequences; it is a situation in which one side of the market cannot observe the other side's actions, also called the hidden action problem. [source](western/市场失灵和微观经济政策.md)
- **Long-run average cost curve**: The long-run average cost curve ($LAC$) is the curve used to describe the relationship between long-run average cost and output. [source](western/生产和成本论.md)
- **Ceiling price**: The ceiling price is the maximum price the government sets for certain daily necessities in order to prevent their prices from rising. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Demand**: Consumers' demand for a good is the quantity of the good they are willing and able to buy at each possible price within a specific period. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Demand-pull inflation**: Demand-pull inflation is a sustained and significant rise in the general price level caused by an increase in aggregate demand. [source](western/通货膨胀理论.md)
- **Cross-price elasticity of demand**: The cross-price elasticity of demand is, within a specific period, the ratio of the percentage change in the quantity demanded of one good or service to the percentage change in the price of another related good or service. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Price elasticity of demand**: The price elasticity of demand reflects the sensitivity of the quantity demanded to a change in price; it is measured by the elasticity coefficient, defined as the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Income elasticity of demand**: The income elasticity of demand is the sensitivity of the quantity demanded to a change in consumer income; its elasticity coefficient is defined as the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in income. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Law of demand**: The law of demand, also called the demand theorem, demand law, or demand principle, states that consumers' quantity demanded changes in the opposite direction to the price of the good. [source](western/需求与供给.md)
- **Budget constraint line**: The budget constraint line, also called the budget line, consumption possibility line, or price line, represents the various combinations of two goods that a consumer's entire income can buy when the consumer's income and the prices of the goods are given. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Income-consumption curve**: The income-consumption curve is the locus of the consumer's utility-maximizing equilibrium points as the consumer's income changes, with the consumer's preferences and the prices of the goods unchanged. It reflects how changes in income alter the quantities consumed. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Cardinal utility theory**: Cardinal utility theory holds that utility can be measured and aggregated with cardinal numbers (1, 2, 3…), and that consumers make choices by comparing the utilities of different bundles of goods. [source](western/效用论.md)
- **Government purchase multiplier**: The government purchase multiplier is the ratio of the change in national income caused by a change in government purchases to the change in government purchases. [source](western/简单国民收入决定理论.md)

## International Economics

- Entries for the international economics section.

- **Law of one price**: The law of one price is a theory about the pricing rule for internationally traded goods under free trade. [source](international/汇率决定的一般理论.md)
- **Dumping**: Dumping is the act of an exporter selling goods on the international market at prices below the domestic market price, even below cost. [source](international/国际贸易政策分析.md)
- **Debt service ratio**: The debt service ratio is the ratio, in a given period, of a country's principal and interest payments on its borrowed external debt to the total of its various export earnings. [source](international/要素的国际流动.md)
- **Customs union**: A customs union is a tariff area formed by two or more countries that ally to become a single tariff area: within the area they mutually abolish tariffs and non-tariff barriers and practice free trade, while imposing a common tariff and trade restrictions on non-member countries. [source](international/经济一体化与国际经济秩序分析.md)
- **Tariff barriers and non-tariff barriers**: Tariff barriers are trade measures that levy tariffs on imported goods, especially high tariffs, in order to protect the domestic market and foster the development of certain domestic industries, thereby restricting the import of foreign goods. [source](international/国际贸易政策分析.md)
- **Premium and discount**: Premium is the counterpart of discount; the two describe the direction in which the forward exchange rate deviates from the spot rate: a premium means a currency's forward exchange rate is above its spot rate, and a discount means the forward rate is below the spot rate; under the direct quotation method, a forward premium on a foreign currency indicates that the foreign currency is rising in value (the forward rate rising), and a discount indicates that it is falling in value (the forward rate falling). [source](international/汇率决定的一般理论.md)
- **Fixed lending and borrowing**: Fixed lending and borrowing refers to the creditor and debtor relationships that have formed in international lending but have not yet entered actual payment. [source](international/汇率决定的一般理论.md)
- **Fixed and floating exchange rates**: A fixed exchange rate is an exchange rate system in which the government chooses a basic reference unit by administrative or legal means and sets, publishes, and maintains a fixed parity between the domestic currency and that reference unit; a floating exchange rate is a rate that rises and falls freely with market supply and demand and which the state has no obligation to maintain; in practice there exist both freely floating and managed floating varieties. [source](international/汇率决定的一般理论.md)
- **International division of labor**: The international division of labor is the division of labor among countries, that is, the international specialization of production. [source](international/绪论.md)
- **Balance of payments**: The concept of the balance of payments has narrow and broad senses. In the narrow sense, the balance of payments refers to the asset transfers between a country and other countries arising from dealings in trade, services, capital, and the like within a given period (generally a year), counting only transactions with present or future foreign exchange receipts and payments; in the broad sense, it refers to the various economic transactions between an economy and the rest of the world in a specific period, including transactions in goods, services, and income, transactions in financial assets and liabilities, and unilateral transfers. [source](international/国际收支分析.md)
- **International economics**: International economics is the branch of economics that studies international economic activities and international economic relations on the basis of the general theory of economics. [source](international/绪论.md)
- **Foreign exchange**: Foreign exchange means the international means of payment or claims held by the monetary authorities (central bank, monetary institutions, foreign exchange stabilization fund organizations, and the treasury) in the form of bank deposits, treasury bills, long- and short-term government securities, and the like, which can be used for payment when the balance of payments is in deficit. [source](international/汇率决定的一般理论.md)
- **Official reserves**: Official reserves are the foreign currency reserve assets and external claims held by a country's central bank or other official monetary institution. [source](international/国际收支分析.md)
- **Infant industry**: If an industry, owing to insufficient technology, low labor productivity, and product costs above world market levels, cannot compete with foreign industries, but under protective measures such as tariffs and subsidies continues production for a period of time and, after that period, becomes able to profit under free trade, reach the level of other countries, stand on its own, and form comparative advantages and develop soundly, such an industry is an infant industry. [source](international/国际贸易政策分析.md)
- **Open economy**: An open economy, also called an "open-type economy," as opposed to a "closed economy," is the economy of a country or region whose economic activities are closely linked with world markets or outside markets (for example, through international trade and international financial dealings). [source](international/绪论.md)
- **Ownership-specific advantages**: Ownership-specific advantages are the advantages a firm possesses in organizational and managerial capability, in financing, in the characteristics and strengths of its technology, in the firm's scale and monopoly position, and in other capabilities. [source](international/要素的国际流动.md)
- **Offer curve**: The offer curve, also called the supply-conditions curve or reciprocal demand curve, was advanced by Marshall and Edgeworth; it shows that for a country to import a certain quantity of goods it must export a certain quantity of goods to other countries, so the offer curve is the locus of the export quantities a country is willing to provide for a given import quantity. [source](international/国际贸易纯理论.md)
- **Comparative advantage**: Comparative advantage gains are the gains from trade obtained when the two trading parties produce according to their respective comparative advantages and then exchange products. [source](international/国际贸易纯理论.md)
- **Exchange rate**: The exchange rate, also called the "parity," the "foreign exchange quotation," or the "exchange market rate," is the price at which foreign exchange is bought and sold. [source](international/汇率决定的一般理论.md)
- **Liquid lending and borrowing**: Liquid lending and borrowing is an important concept in the international lending theory, a theory of exchange rate determination in international finance. [source](international/汇率决定的一般理论.md)
- **Present-value debt ratio**: The present-value debt ratio is the ratio of the present value of a debtor country's outstanding debt in the current year to its GNP (or exports) that year; it is divided into the economic present-value debt ratio (the present value of outstanding debt as a share of that year's GNP, with a critical value of 80%) and the export present-value debt ratio (the present value of outstanding debt as a share of that year's exports, with a critical value of 20%). [source](international/要素的国际流动.md)

- **Dual comparison coefficient**: The dual comparison coefficient is the ratio of agriculture's comparative labor productivity to non-agriculture's comparative labor productivity in a dual economic structure. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的经济结构调整.md)
- **Dual economic structure**: A dual economic structure is an economic structure in which a modern sector dominated by urban industry and a traditional sector dominated by rural agriculture coexist, the traditional sector's share being excessively large, the modern sector underdeveloped, and the urban–rural gap very pronounced. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的经济结构调整.md)
- **Industry**: An industry is a series of enterprises producing similar or identical products. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的经济结构调整.md)
- **Industrial structure**: Industrial structure refers to the economic linkages and the quantitative proportional relations formed in the process of reproduction among the various industries within the national economy. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的经济结构调整.md)
- **Property rights**: Property rights are rights, realized through social enforcement, to choose among the various uses of some economic good. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Property rights system**: A property rights system is an institutional arrangement formed by the combination of given property rights relations and property rights rules, able to combine, regulate and protect property rights relations effectively. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Property rights rule**: The property rights rule is that the size of a person's power over the allocation of resources is positively correlated with the quantity of assets the person owns — the more assets owned, the greater the power to allocate resources. [source](socialist/向社会主义市场经济体制的渐进过渡.md)
- **Agency costs**: Agency costs are the costs that the introduction of agency necessarily induces, under the condition that ownership and control are separated, because the utility functions of principal and agent are not fully identical. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Agency problem**: Besides pursuing higher monetary returns, the agent also strives, through the pursuit of non-monetary goods, to obtain as many non-monetary gains as possible. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Enterprise co-governance**: Enterprise co-governance emphasizes joint participation in decision-making and mutual restraint in supervision. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Ownership of the enterprise**: Ownership of the enterprise denotes the rights of the enterprise, over the property it lawfully possesses, to use it, to enjoy its proceeds and to dispose of it. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Enterprise governance structure**: The enterprise governance structure, also called the corporate legal-person governance structure, is the unified mechanism — institutionalized by law, regulations, rules and the company charter — through which owners, managers and supervisors form, by way of the company's organ of power (the shareholders' meeting), the organs of business decision-making and execution (the board of directors and the managers), and the organ of supervision (the board of supervisors), relations with clearly defined powers and responsibilities, mutual restraint, coordinated operation and scientific decision-making. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Right of use**: The right of use is the possibility of utilizing property according to its purpose without changing the nature of its ownership or possession; it is the rights-and-obligations relation arising between people from the utilization of property. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Supply-dominated institutional change**: Supply-dominated institutional change is compulsory institutional change pushed forward by the power centre; it means that, under a given constitutional order and the ethical norms of conduct, the power centre's capacity and willingness to provide new institutional arrangements are the dominant factors determining institutional change, and that this capacity and willingness are determined mainly by the power structure and the balance of forces among the various vested-interest groups of a society. [source](socialist/向社会主义市场经济体制的渐进过渡.md)
- **Full employment**: Full employment is that economic state in which every worker willing to work finds employment at a wage he or she can accept. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的政府调节.md)
- **Public goods**: Public goods are those products whose consumption has neither exclusivity nor exhaustibility but for which charging is difficult. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Equalization of public services**: Equalization of public services means chiefly that all citizens enjoy equal opportunity of access to basic public services and broadly equal results, while their freedom of choice as members of society is respected. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的分配制度.md)
- **Insider control**: Insider control refers to the gradual acquisition, by the managers of state-owned enterprises in the course of economic transition, of the greater part of the control rights — an acquisition often accomplished through "collusion" with the workers. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Institutions**: Institutions are a series of devised rules, compliance procedures and ethical-moral norms of behavior, intended to constrain the behavior of individuals in pursuit of the maximization of their own welfare or utility. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Institutional arrangement**: An institutional arrangement is a series of concrete operating rules that, under the constitutional order, constrain particular patterns of behavior and relations and define the conditions of exchange. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Incentive-compatibility constraint**: The incentive-compatibility constraint states that, since the agent is a taker of the contract, the incentives the mechanism provides must be able to induce the agent to choose voluntarily the contract designed for the type to which he or she belongs. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Right of possession**: The right of possession is the possibility of a person exercising direct control over property; it is the rights-and-obligations relation arising between the owner and others from actual control of the property. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Participation constraint**: The participation constraint, also called the individual-rationality constraint, imposes a rationalized assumption upon the agent's behavior. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Sustainable development**: The theory of sustainable development is a major advance in humankind's view of development, stressing the coordinated development of the economy, society, resources and environmental protection. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Gross domestic product (GDP)**: Gross domestic product is the monetary expression of the total final goods and services produced, within a country's territory over a given period (usually a year), by that country's own residents and by foreign residents alike. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Gross national product (GNP)**: Gross national product is the sum of the value of all final goods and services produced over a given period (usually a year) by the resident (national) population of a country (or region); it is accounted on the national principle, and is distinguished from GDP, which is accounted by territorial extent. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Basic industries**: Basic industries are the group of industries that occupy a foundational position in a country's national economic development, constraining and determining the development of other industries and setting the level they attain; they include basic industry and infrastructure, their products usually becoming indispensable inputs or consumables in the processing, reprocessing and production processes of the downstream sectors, and their investment usually being of huge magnitude and indivisible. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Incremental reform**: Incremental reform means advancing market-oriented reform at the margin without being the first to disturb the existing pattern of vested interests — that is to say, on the margin where hierarchical rules act less, choosing ways of adjusting interests that carry the meaning of Pareto improvements, so as to carry out systemic change and pass gradually toward a market economy system. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济理论.md)
- **Right of disposition**: The right of disposition is the possibility, guaranteed by law, of performing acts aimed at changing the economic use or the state of property; it reflects the rights-and-obligations relations that arise in the process of a person altering property. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Export-oriented industrialization strategy**: The basic import of the export-oriented industrialization strategy is to make use of opening up and of the global trend of industrial restructuring and transfer, building China's model of industrialization on the advantage of labor costs against the background of openness. [source](socialist/社会主义对外经济关系.md)
- **Equilibrium of external constraint**: A rational principal will set the external constraint upon the agent at the level where the marginal cost of constraint equals the agency cost; this process of adjustment toward equilibrium will minimize the sum of the constraint cost and the agency cost. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Macro income distribution process**: The macro-level process of income regulation is a redistribution process built upon the micro income distribution process and independent of that distribution process. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的分配制度.md)
- **Constitutional order**: The constitutional order is the basic structure used to define the state's property rights and control; it comprises the whole set of fundamental political, social and legal rules that establish production, exchange and distribution, and it establishes the principles for collective choice, being thus the rule for making rules. [source](socialist/向社会主义市场经济体制的渐进过渡.md)
- **Market**: The market is the venue, the channel and the bond of exchange. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济理论.md)
- **Market mechanism**: The market mechanism is an organic system of economic operation and regulation formed within market trading relations, resting on the three-in-one interactive relation of price, supply and demand, and competition. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济理论.md)
- **Market economy**: A market economy is an economy in which the market mechanism allocates resources. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济理论.md)
- **Micro income distribution process**: The micro income distribution process is realized through the workings of the market mechanism. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的分配制度.md)
- **Ownership**: In Marx's definition, ownership determines the ultimate attribution of a thing and signifies the property right of a subject to the exclusive and monopolistic holding of a definite thing — a property right existing independently, in respect of the same thing, of any other rights. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Technological progress**: Technological progress is the process of motion by which people, using more efficient means of labor and techniques of production, propel the development of society's productive forces. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的经济结构调整.md)
- **Distribution according to work**: Distribution according to work is the system of distribution under which, after the necessary deductions have been made from the total social product, personal consumption goods are distributed according to the effective amount of labor each worker supplies to society — more work, more gain; less work, less gain; no work, no gain. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的分配制度.md)
- **Right to the proceeds**: The right to the proceeds is the possibility of obtaining the economic benefits arising from the owner's property; it is the rights-and-obligations relation people enter into through acquiring additional property. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Comparative labor productivity**: Comparative labor productivity is the ratio of a sector's share of output value (or of income) to the share of the labor force employed in that sector. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的经济结构调整.md)
- **Legal-person enterprise system**: By the form in which property is organized and the legal liability borne, the organizational forms of enterprises divide into natural-person enterprises and legal-person enterprises; the legal-person enterprise system is the enterprise system that takes the legal-person enterprise as its organizational form: the enterprise owns independent legal-person property and bears civil liability independently with all of its property, the contributors bear limited liability up to the amount of their contributions, and the company is its typical form. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Mixed-ownership economy**: A mixed-ownership economy is an economic form composed of ownership economies of different natures joined together. [source](socialist/社会主义经济制度的本质特征.md)
- **Gradualist reform**: Gradualist reform is market-oriented reform carried out on the foundation of industrialization and the socialist constitutional system, which emphasizes making use of the organizational resources already in hand to advance reform, and which implements incremental reform on the premise of not basically disturbing the existing pattern of vested interests. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济理论.md)
- **Core of incentives**: The core of incentives is to convert the agent's pursuit of personal utility into a pursuit of the maximization of enterprise profit. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Price stability**: Price stability means not the stability of the relative prices among the various goods and factors, but the stability of the general price level across the whole of society. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的政府调节.md)
- **Contingent governance mechanism**: The contingent governance mechanism is the institutionalization of the state-contingency of enterprise ownership: different states of the enterprise's operation correspond to different arrangements of ownership over the enterprise. [source](socialist/国有企业治理结构的创新.md)
- **Primary stage of socialism**: The primary stage of socialism is the particular historical stage through which building socialism must necessarily pass under the conditions of backward productive forces and an underdeveloped commodity economy in our country — the entire historical stage from China's entry into socialism to the basic realization of socialist modernization. [source](socialist/社会主义经济制度的本质特征.md)
- **Social security**: Social security is a system by which the state and society distribute and redistribute the national income through legislation, so as to provide basic living guarantees for the members of society, especially for individuals or families in special difficulty of livelihood. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的分配制度.md)
- **Competitive state-owned enterprises**: Competitive state-owned enterprises are those state-owned enterprises built with state investment which face essentially no barriers to entry or exit, in whose industry numerous enterprises exist, whose products are essentially homogeneous and divisible, and whose goal of operation is profit. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Hierarchical rules**: Hierarchical rules mean that first a pyramid-shaped hierarchy of layer-upon-layer subordination is constructed, then each actor's position within that hierarchy is defined, and then, further, the power to allocate resources suited to that hierarchical position is defined. [source](socialist/向社会主义市场经济体制的渐进过渡.md)

[source](socialist/社会主义对外经济关系.md)
- **Economic development**: Economic development is the evolutionary process of a country's or a region's economic growth together with the continuous optimization and upgrading of its economic structure and social structure. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Economic development model**: An economic development model is the particular type formed, over a given period, by the national economic development strategy together with its mechanism for the growth of the productive factors and its principles of operation; it comprises a whole series of elements, including the goals, the manner, the focal points and the steps of development. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Economic growth**: Economic growth is the long-run increase in the real output (or the real income) of an economic society — that is, the increase in full-employment output measured at a constant price level. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Transformation of the mode of economic growth**: The transformation of the mode of economic growth means that economic growth turns from depending mainly on the quantitative expansion of the factors of production to being achieved mainly by raising the efficiency with which the input factors of production are used — that is, the transformation from the extensive to the intensive mode of growth. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Economic openness**: Economic openness means that an economic system establishes contacts with the outside world through the flow of products, services, technology, factors and so on. [source](socialist/社会主义对外经济关系.md)
- **Economic policy instruments**: Economic policy instruments are the methods the state adopts in order to achieve its economic policy goals; they comprise the policy tools and the methods of implementing policy. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的政府调节.md)
- **Economic-legal means**: The economic-legal means are the means by which the state relies on the coercive force of law to guarantee the achievement of its economic policy goals. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的政府调节.md)
- **Green GDP**: In 1993 the relevant statistical bodies of the United Nations put forward the concept of the ecologically adjusted domestic product, "EDP" — that is, green GDP, which is GDP minus the value of the resources consumed in creating GDP, and then minus the cost of treating the pollution created in producing GDP. - **Joint-stock company**: A joint-stock company is a company initiated and organized by a number of shareholders above a certain minimum, whose entire capital is divided into shares of equal value, whose shareholders bear limited liability toward the company up to the shares they have subscribed, and whose stock may be publicly issued and freely transferred in society. - **Natural-person enterprise system**: By the form in which property is organized and the legal liability borne, the organizational forms of enterprises divide into natural-person enterprises and legal-person enterprises; the natural-person enterprise system is the enterprise system that takes the natural-person enterprise as its organizational form: the enterprise does not have the status of a legal person, and the proprietor bears unlimited liability for the enterprise's debts, the single-proprietorship and the partnership being its typical forms. [source](socialist/社会主义企业制度与国有企业改革.md)
- **Ethical and moral norms of behavior**: Ethical and moral norms of behavior spring from people's understanding of reality and from ideology, and are bound up with judgments of the justice or fairness of actual contractual relations; they are of vital importance for legitimizing the constitutional order and institutional arrangements. [source](socialist/社会主义经济增长与经济发展.md)
- **Managerial control under administrative intervention**: China's reform of the state-owned enterprises has mainly redistributed powers, responsibilities and interests through the marketization and contractualization of the relations between government and enterprise: the government grants the managers very wide managerial powers while monitoring their conduct, thereby forming a type of enterprise governance structure — managerial control under administrative intervention — distinct from insider control. - **Administrative control means**: Administrative control means are the means by which the state's administrative departments, relying on the might of state power, intervene in economic life through the issuance of orders, directives and the like. [source](socialist/社会主义市场经济条件下的政府调节.md)
